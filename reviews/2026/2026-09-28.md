### 🟠 medium — deps.js treats a failed `npm audit` as "no known issues"
`src/deps.js:53-67` · commit `5ec51fd`

`npm audit --json` doesn't always fail by producing invalid JSON — on network/registry errors it commonly exits non-zero while still printing valid JSON of the form `{"error": {...}}` with no `metadata.vulnerabilities`. In that case `audit` is truthy, so `auditFailed: !audit` is `false`, `counts` becomes `{}`, and the report shows "✅ bez známých chyb" even though the audit never actually ran — exactly the false sense of safety the commit's own comment says it wants to avoid. Fix by also treating a present `audit.error` (or missing `audit.metadata`) as a failed audit, e.g. `auditFailed: !audit || !!audit.error || !audit.metadata`.
