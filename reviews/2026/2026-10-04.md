## wisp-buddy

### 🟡 Low — Reminder text goes to Telegram by default, and failures are silent
`src-tauri/src/reminders.rs:153` · commit `6b3fa9b`
`phone` defaults to `true`, so reminder text is forwarded to Wisp's Telegram as soon as it is updated, with no opt-in. `notify()` returns `false` when Wisp is down or answers non-200, and the result is dropped. A reminder meant for the phone then fails without any sign. Default `phone` to `false` (opt-in). Also log or show the result of `notify()`, for example fall back to the Mac notification path.

### 🟡 Low — Nearby mouse movement re-rolls the jump chance every frame
`src-tauri/src/pet.rs:393-411` · commit `fa7394a`
While the mouse keeps moving, `p.until = now` is set again on every tick, and each tick re-rolls `jump_chance()` (raised to 0.25 in `2e1750e`) before `next_activity` can pick "walk to the mouse". By my estimate roughly a third of these encounters end in a jump onto a window instead of a walk to the mouse. That is the opposite of what the commit intends. Skip the jump roll when the early wake-up fired, or apply the early wake-up only once per rest.

### 🟡 Low — The new config write path has no tests
`src-tauri/src/config.rs:30-48`, `src-tauri/src/reminders.rs:92-103` · commit `2e1750e`
`RUST_OWNED` merging in `config_save` and the take-and-fire step in `change()` for due and repeating reminders are new logic that decides which side wins and when a reminder is dropped.
