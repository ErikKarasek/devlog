## Highlights

- Shipped a dynamic macOS notch overlay for Dispečink showing real-time Claude, ChatGPT, and Gemini usage rings, live agent steps, and permission prompts answerable directly from the notch.
- Integrated ChatGPT (via Codex CLI) and Gemini (via Antigravity/`agy`) as first-class agent engines alongside Claude, with per-engine tagging throughout the UI.
