# 2026-10-01 — Thursday

**TL;DR:** Massive day shipping the Wisp agent-dashboard ecosystem — a renamed Mac menubar app, a full SwiftUI iPhone app with widgets, a Cloudflare relay, and a nightly review pipeline that auto-files fix tasks and sends Telegram notifications.

## Highlights
- Shipped a complete SwiftUI iPhone app (sideloaded via SideStore) for the Wisp/dispecink agent dashboard, including Dynamic Island, lock-screen card, four widget variants, and deep-link navigation — all synced to the Mac via a Cloudflare relay.
- Built a fully animated notch UI ("Grok Bot"-style) with six body shapes, 40 characters, sliding shimmer steps, mood states, drag-to-ask, and per-agent persona switching on both Mac and iPhone.
- Renamed the entire product from dispecink to **Wisp** across Mac app, notch, menus, Telegram, and iPhone — keeping all internal identifiers, keys, and relay paths stable.
- Hardened the nightly review pipeline: serious findings now become Fixer agent tasks (max 6, no duplicates), Telegram reports what Fixer received, and runs trigger per review rather than once per day.
- Built an inbox sweep for job-mail: spam and ads trashed by sender, domain-level blocking, casino-scam detection by content, and a `/uklid`/`/blokovane`/`/odblokuj` Telegram command set.
